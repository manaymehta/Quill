## Write

Markdown without the noise. The marks appear only on the line your cursor is on, so **a page reads like a page** until you need to change it.

  • Checklists, tags, and folders inside folders
  • Links that open into little preview cards
  • Up to twelve notes open at once, as tabs in the dock
  • Full screen, where the dock shrinks to a dot. That one, below.

## Ask

Ask a question and Quill answers from your own notes, citing the ones it used. It even reads the changes you haven't saved yet.

## Tidy

Say “archive everything untitled” or “move my #react notes into Frontend”. Quill lists every change with its own tick box. Apply once, and undo for ten days.

## What it won't do

  • Save anything without your click
  • Delete a note for good, at any setting
  • Undo over a note you changed after it did
> Your notes stay the source of truth. The agent only ever proposes.

Summary

Quill is a notes app with an agent that reads your notes and answers from them, citing what it used.

It writes in markdown that stays out of your way, keeps up to twelve notes open in a dock, and draws your notes as a graph.

Every change the agent suggests waits for your yes, and applied changes can be undone for ten days.
