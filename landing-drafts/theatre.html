Every shared tag is a line between two notes.

The graph draws every note as a point and every shared tag as a line. Pick a tag and its corner of your thinking lights up.

Ask the agent to “group my college notes that feel related” and it suggests folders, each one yours to accept or leave.

The agent works inside your notes and nowhere else. It reads what you can read, and it never acts without you.

It can
  • Search your notes and cite the ones it used
  • Draft notes and edits as unsaved tabs
  • Propose moves, tags and archives as a review list
  • Group related notes into suggested folders
It can’t
  • Save anything without your click
  • Delete a note for good, at any setting
  • Undo over a note you changed after it
  • Reach anything outside Quill
