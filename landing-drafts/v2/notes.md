# Quill landing page — working notes (taste skill run)

## Brief
- Subject: Quill, a notes app (MERN + an AI agent). Live app at quill-green.vercel.app.
- Audience: anyone who arrives at the site. Actions: Sign up (primary, `/signup`), Log in (`/login`).
- Brand kept: charcoal page `#202124` (the mark is drawn for `#28271f`), cream paper `#f4eadc` / `#f8ecdc`,
  one coral `#e85d56` (hover `#fb6d65`), Playfair Display (titles), Poppins (interface), Lora (writing).
- Logo: `design/quill-mark/v22`: a glowing coral line quill whose feather is a constellation of cream
  stars; draws itself in (~3.3s), then a grainy pool of light spreads under the nib. Made for a dark ground.
- Imagery that truly exists: the real app interface (can be built in HTML or screenshotted from the
  running app); the quill mark. No photographs of people or places of its own.

## Inventory (things only Quill has)
1. The name: a quill, the oldest pen; the mark draws itself like ink.
2. The feather is a constellation: its stars are notes, its lines are links. The app's own background
   is the same constellation, drifting behind every page.
3. The pool of light under the nib: the one place being written right now.
4. Cream paper notes on a charcoal desk; one coral for the thing to press.
5. The editor: Playfair title, Lora text, markdown marks that show only on the line the cursor is on.
6. The dock: up to 12 notes open as tabs; in full screen it shrinks to a single coral dot.
7. The agent answers from your notes and shows which notes it used; it reads unsaved changes too.
8. Bulk changes arrive as a review list: one tick box per change, one Apply, Undo for 10 days.
9. It never deletes anything for good; Undo never overwrites a note you changed afterwards.
10. The graph: notes as points, shared tags as lines (the constellation again).
11. The first note every account gets: "Welcome to Quill!" with a feather.
12. Numbers: 12 open tabs, 10 chats, 10 days of undo, 50 notes per review list.

## Argument (claims → proof)
0. Hero. Quill is a place to write that gives your notes back when you ask.  → the mark, large, drawing itself; the product beside it; Sign up.
1. Writing stays quiet.                       → the editor, large and real.
2. Ask, and it answers from your own notes.   → a real answer with its sources.
3. Big tidy-ups wait for your yes.            → a real review list; undo for 10 days; nothing deleted for good.
4. Your notes connect into a map.             → the graph, which is the constellation in the mark.
5. Start.                                     → the name at full width, Sign up, Log in.
