03 · user guide

frequently questioned responses.

is my note content processed securely?

yes. your note contents are processed temporarily in-memory when summarizing or analyzing connections. your documents are housed securely in isolated databases and are never used to train external models.

how does the map link my notes?

Quill monitors the hashtags you attach to notes. when multiple notes share identical hashtags, the interactive mind map automatically draws connection lines between them to construct your knowledge network.

does the editor support text formatting?

yes. our note editor supports live markdown editing. you can format headings, build interactive checklists, write links, and structure lists with real-time styling that updates automatically as you type.
